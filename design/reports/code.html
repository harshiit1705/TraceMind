Postmortem: INC-8492 — Aurora DB Connection Pool Exhaustion

Commander: Alex Chen (Staff SRE)
•
Scribe: autonomous-agent-4
•
Incident Date: Oct 24, 2024 (14:18 - 14:35 UTC)
01

Executive Summary

On October 24, at 14:18 UTC, a cascade of 504 Gateway Timeouts occurred on core checkout endpoints. The root cause was an uncommitted batch query holding exclusive row-level locks on orders_ledger, leading to 980 of 1,000 pool connections becoming blocked. Full recovery was achieved at 14:35 UTC following rapid termination of zombie connections and dynamic connection limit adjustments.
